###RAN: Tsarstvo Lisy, Ran Yakumo's realm in the north###
#Design: .claude/docs/ran_tree.html (5 columns). Every mission is one of Yukari's requests to Ran.
#Hidden state used across the tree:
#	ran_own_will (country variable)	Ran's own will, never shown. Refusing an Offer +2, milestones +1. Read by the finale.
#	ran_gap_touched (province flag)	Yukari's gaps were used here. More loyalists spawn here in the finale (at most 10 armies).
#	ran_refused_yukari, ran_civil_war_started, ran_civil_war_over, ran_lost_civil_war (country flags)	The finale and the civil war (th_ran_civil_war).
#	ran_chen_in_the_north (country flag)	Chen serves Ran (RAN_chen_comes_north). She stays loyal in the finale.
#	ran_letter_received, ran_chen_mischief_seen (country flags)	Cooldowns of the Letters from Mayohiga and Chen's Mischief (flavor_ran.99).
#	ran_fox_charmed (country flag, on others)	Set by the Fox's Charm action; needed by the Fox's Revelation and the HRE missions.
#Events: events/flavorRAN.txt (flavor_ran.10-19 column 1, 20-29 column 2, 30-39 column 3, 40-49 column 4, 50-59 column 5, 60-83 the finale, 85-99 the recurring events). Modifiers: common/event_modifiers/th_ran_modifiers.txt.
#Icons: mission_ran_* sprites in interface/missionicons_touhou.gfx (art supplied by the user).
